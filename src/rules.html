Experience RolePlay has no restrictions; simply observe these five core rules.

01
CORE RULE

Lore RP

Respect the server's lore, storyline, factions, and world-building. Your character must fit naturally within the universe of Experience RP.

02
CORE RULE

Quality RP

Every interaction should contribute to the roleplay. Avoid low-effort, troll, or unrealistic RP that negatively affects the experience of others.

03
CORE RULE

Logic RP

Use common sense and realistic decision-making. Your character's actions must make sense within the situation and carry believable consequences.

04
CORE RULE

No Metagaming

Do not use information obtained outside of RP to influence your character's decisions, actions, or knowledge in-game.

05
CORE RULE

No Exploits

No duping, glitching, hacking, or exploiting bugs. Any discovered exploit must be reported to the staff team immediately.
