The beginning

The Day Everything Changed

July 10, 2026. It began as an ordinary morning in a city that had no idea it was living through its final hours of normality.

12:03 PM

The alarm

The message goes silent

Television broadcasts cut out. A single warning orders every citizen indoors, without explanation. Minutes later, military vehicles, tanks and fighter jets seal the city behind barricades.

15:30

The silent city

Three days under lockdown

The streets empty. Radios repeat the same command while distant engines, screams, explosions and gunfire move through the dark. Behind restricted walls, a secret war becomes a race against time.

Day four / 06:00 AM

The evacuation

Run to the shelters

Sirens wake the city. Families are ordered outside and sent toward shelters as panic swallows the streets. Then black smoke rises above the skyline, followed by a flash that changes everything.

Twenty years later

The vault opens

Time to go outside

Twenty years after the disaster, the sealed vault door unexpectedly unlocked. Hazard-suited figures entered the bunker and silently observed the survivors before delivering a single command: it was finally time to go outside.

The survivors were divided into groups and escorted through the underground tunnels toward the surface. When they emerged, they found a world beyond recognition — abandoned vehicles, ruined buildings, dust-covered roads, and collapsed structures stretching across the remains of the city.

Their mysterious guides led them toward a protected zone before disappearing without explanation. No identities were revealed. No answers were given. After twenty years underground, the survivors were finally standing beneath an open sky, facing a world they no longer recognized.
